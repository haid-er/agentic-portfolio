## 1. HumCareADL: A diverse multimodal dataset and HyMCL-Net for robust human activity recognition ✅
- **Authors:** Ayesha Ashraf, **Malik Haider Ali**, Nazish Ashfaq, Muhammad Hassan Khan, Muhammad Shahid Farid
- **Affiliation:** Faculty of Computing & IT, University of the Punjab, Lahore
- **Journal:** *Multimedia Tools and Applications* (Springer), Vol. 85, Issue 9, Article 731 (2026); Q1 per co-author / SJR ~0.78 ⚠️ indicative
- **Published online:** 3 September 2026
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s11042-026-21905-3 — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11042-026-21905-3
- **Contributions:** (1) HumCareADL — diverse multimodal wearable-IMU HAR dataset spanning varied age groups and contexts, addressing limited demographics / low sampling rates / small cohorts of prior datasets; (2) HyMCL-Net — hybrid multi-branch CNN-LSTM (CNN spatial features + LSTM temporal dependencies).
- **Results:** 85.27% avg accuracy on HumCareADL; 81.83% on CogAge; 98.52% on WISDM.
